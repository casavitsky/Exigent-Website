Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of searches can you help with?

Any search. We specialize in supply chain, procurement and operations roles in construction, manufacturing, food and beverage, and hospitality, and we take on myriad searches outside those areas too, including for ExigentHR's payroll and HR clients.

How much does it cost to use a recruiter?

For contingency searches, you pay a fee only when you hire someone we present. Retained searches are priced by engagement.

How long does a search take?

We deliver a first shortlist in 2-3 business days. Time to hire depends on the role, the market and your interview schedule.

What's the difference between contingency and retained search?

Contingency means you pay only if you hire. Retained means you pay part of the fee up front for a dedicated search, which suits senior or confidential roles.

Do you recruit outside Florida?

Yes. We're based in St. Petersburg, Florida, and recruit for companies across the U.S.

What if a hire doesn't work out?

Our placements come with a replacement guarantee. If a hire doesn't work out, we run the search again at no added fee. Certain restrictions apply.

Can you help with onboarding and payroll after the hire?

Yes. ExigentHR also provides payroll, HR, benefits and insurance, so new hires can be onboarded through the same team.
