About Us

We've Built This Before

The people behind ExigentHR helped grow Modern Business Associates (MBA) from a start-up into one of the largest privately held PEOs in the United States: 46,000 worksite employees, 42 states and more than $1 billion in annual payroll.

ExigentHR brings that team back together, on a modern platform and with more under one roof: payroll, HR outsourcing, benefits, business insurance and recruiting. One relationship, everything handled.
