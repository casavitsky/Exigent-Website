Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

ExigentHR (“ExigentHR,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and safeguard personal information obtained through our website, services, communications, and business operations.

Information We Collect

Depending on your relationship with ExigentHR and the services being provided, we may collect personal information including:

  • Name, mailing address, email address, telephone number, and other contact information
  • Employment and job-related information, including employment history, job title, compensation, and work location
  • Social Security numbers, taxpayer identification numbers, dates of birth, and other identifying information
  • Payroll, tax withholding, direct deposit, banking, and other financial information
  • Benefits enrollment and eligibility information, including information regarding dependents and beneficiaries
  • Information necessary to administer human resources, payroll, benefits, workers’ compensation, and related services
  • Information submitted through employment applications, onboarding forms, service requests, or other communications
  • Device, browser, IP address, website usage, cookie, and similar technical information
  • Mobile telephone numbers and communication preferences when you elect to receive SMS communications

We seek to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for legitimate business, employment, service, or legal purposes.

How We Collect Information

We may collect personal information directly from you, from your employer or prospective employer, from clients and business partners, from authorized third parties, and through your interaction with our website and services.

Information may be collected through website forms, employment applications, employee onboarding, payroll and benefits enrollment, customer service requests, electronic communications, SMS opt-ins, integrations with third-party systems, cookies, and similar technologies.

How We Use Information

ExigentHR may use personal information to:

  • Provide payroll processing, human resources, employee benefits, workforce administration, and related services
  • Process payroll, tax filings, deductions, direct deposits, and other authorized transactions
  • Administer employee onboarding, benefits, workers’ compensation, and other employment-related programs
  • Verify identity, employment information, and employment eligibility
  • Communicate with clients, employees, applicants, business partners, and website users
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Provide service-related communications and notifications
  • Maintain business, payroll, tax, employment, and compliance records
  • Protect the security and integrity of our systems, services, clients, and users
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, misuse, security incidents, or other unlawful activity
  • Improve our website, services, technology, and business operations
  • Comply with applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations, legal processes, and governmental requirements

We do not use personal information for purposes materially different from those described in this Privacy Policy without appropriate notice or authorization when required.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or SMS consent with third parties for marketing purposes. Your mobile number and SMS consent information are used only for communications related to our services, such as support updates, appointment reminders, and service notifications.

Your consent to receive SMS communications is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing or receiving services from ExigentHR.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly at [[INFO-EMAIL]] or 727.634.0660.

Mobile information and SMS consent will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may disclose information to service providers that facilitate SMS communications on our behalf solely as necessary to provide those communications and subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection requirements.

ExigentHR may disclose personal information when reasonably necessary to provide our services or operate our business. Recipients may include:

  • Payroll, benefits, banking, insurance, technology, data processing, communications, and other service providers that support our operations
  • Clients and employers when necessary to provide contracted services
  • Government agencies, taxing authorities, regulators, courts, or law enforcement agencies when required or permitted by law

We expect service providers that receive personal information on our behalf to use appropriate safeguards and to process information only for authorized purposes.
